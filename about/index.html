About us

A hospital for our people.

Rooted in Entebbe. Committed to the health and wellbeing of the communities we serve.

Our purpose

Care grounded in community.

Entebbe Regional Referral Hospital is a public healthcare facility serving Entebbe and surrounding communities. Every day, our teams work across outpatient, inpatient and specialist care to support people at their most important moments.

We are focused on a welcoming, supportive experience for patients and their families — and on working with partners to keep improving care.
