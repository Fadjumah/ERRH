How patients reach us from health centres and district facilities, and how emergency care works day and night.

For referring facilities

Referring a patient to us.

We receive patients referred from health centre IVs, health centre IIIs, district facilities and private clinics across the region, as well as patients who arrive directly in an emergency.

01

Send the referral

Referring clinicians complete the standard referral form with the diagnosis, treatment given so far and reason for referral.

02

Call ahead

Phone the hospital before transferring a patient so the receiving team and any critical care support can be prepared.

03

Arrive at casualty

All referred and emergency patients are received at the accident & emergency unit for triage, day or night.

04

Feedback to the facility

After treatment, patients are discharged back to their nearest facility with notes for continued follow-up care.

Critical care support

Ready for the patients others refer.

24/7 casualty & resuscitation

Emergency assessment and stabilisation for patients arriving by ambulance or on foot.

Medical oxygen supply

Piped and cylinder oxygen supporting theatre, critical care and newborn units.

Blood transfusion support

Safe blood supply for surgery, maternity emergencies and severe anaemia.

Round-the-clock diagnostics

Laboratory and imaging support so treatment decisions are not delayed.
