Outpatient clinic days

When each clinic runs.

Specialist outpatient clinics run on set days so you meet the right team. Emergency and casualty care is available at all times, including weekends.

MondayChronic care: hypertension, diabetes & general medicine review
TuesdayPaediatrics, newborn follow-up & child health
WednesdaySurgery, orthopaedics & trauma review
ThursdayAntenatal, high-risk pregnancy & gynaecology
FridayEye, dental, ENT & mental health
Saturday & SundayEmergency and casualty care only

Clinic days can change with staffing and emergencies. Please call the hospital to confirm before travelling.

Patients’ charter

Your rights and responsibilities.

As a public hospital we follow the national patients’ charter. It sets out what you can expect from us, and how we work together with you and your family.

As a patient, you have the right

  • To be treated with dignity, courtesy and respect, whoever you are.
  • To have your condition and treatment explained in a language you understand.
  • To give or withhold consent before any procedure.
  • To privacy and confidentiality of your health information.
  • To know the name and role of the staff caring for you.
  • To raise a complaint or give feedback without fear.

We ask that you

  • Bring your referral letter, patient card and any previous results.
  • Share your full health history honestly with the care team.
  • Follow treatment advice and keep your follow-up appointments.
  • Treat staff and other patients with respect and keep wards calm.
  • Help keep the hospital clean and observe infection prevention guidance.

Feedback, questions or a complaint?

Speak to the nurse in charge of your ward or ask for the hospital administration. Your feedback helps us improve care for everyone.
